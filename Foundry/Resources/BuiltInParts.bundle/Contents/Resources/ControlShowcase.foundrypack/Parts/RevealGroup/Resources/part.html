Reveal group

Animates an element as it enters the viewport. Unlike the CSS groups, Reveal consumes its own values: no template interpolation is needed, and its values are exposed only so a part can reflect them elsewhere.

Reveal

The full group, with no targetSelector, animates the part root — this whole sheet. Enable Reveal, then scroll it out of view and back in Preview. The box below borrows revealDuration for its own hover transition.

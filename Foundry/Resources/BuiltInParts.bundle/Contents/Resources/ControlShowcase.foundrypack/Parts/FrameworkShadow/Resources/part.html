Framework Shadow

A CSS-ready box-shadow string. Framework tokens resolve through the active framework; custom layers join as a comma-separated list, and no layers gives none.

Framework shadow

defaults.base is a predefined ID: none, inner, xs, sm, md, lg, xl or 2xl. The picker also lists the project's custom shadows.

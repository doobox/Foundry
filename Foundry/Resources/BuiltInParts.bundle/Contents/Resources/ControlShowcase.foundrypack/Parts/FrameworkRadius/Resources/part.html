Control · frameworkRadius

Framework Radius

Four corners from the framework's radius scale. Printed directly it is the border-radius shorthand; each corner, its amount and its unit are also available.

Shared token

defaults.base as one token gives every corner the same var(--foundry-border-radius-…) reference and starts the ring linked. Framework amounts resolve in px.

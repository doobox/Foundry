Date

A date-only value with no time or timezone. Print it as YYYY-MM-DD, read its calendar parts, or format it for readers.

Picker

The default form. Beyond the raw value, year, month and day are numbers, monthName and weekday are English names, and the formatDate, addDays and addMonths filters transform the output only.

Control · video

Video

A project video with its playback options built in. The direct value is its URL, empty until one is chosen; a video bound to it becomes a drop well on the canvas.

Standard element

label and "defaults": {"base": ""}. The element takes src, poster and the ready-made attributes list; the canvas shows the poster and never streams the movie.
